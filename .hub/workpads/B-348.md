## Implementation

`dictionary._values_look_yearish()` renders each nonmissing whole numeric float
as its integer, matching R's existing `as.character()` year rule. Fractional
floats and decimal text keep their spelling and fail the year pattern. The
normalization stays inside this predicate, leaving `_character_values()` and
its numericish caller unchanged. Empty and infinite inputs remain non-yearish.

The CSV regression uses exactly `BY,n\n2001,1\n,2\n2003,3\n`, proves pandas
stored `BY` as float64 with one blank cell, asserts the private predicate before
the public role, and independently asserts the public role to expose both RED
failures. Additional controls cover nullable Float64, float32 and object-stored
numeric floats; float spawner counts retain the measurement role/invariant.
The old test header's float-gap claim is replaced with the now-ported rule.

R source at canonical `3fd7e76` is unchanged. A paired probe read the CSV with
readr and compared eight predicate cases plus CSV and measurement roles; all
ten outputs agree with Python 3.13.11/pandas 3.0.6 and with Python 3.9.6/pandas
1.5.3. Numeric `1850.5`, text `"1850.0"`, out-of-range years, infinity and an
empty column remain false on both sides.
