So the item's warning about its own wording was right, and both halves of it are

* **Overstated:** the word *spreadsheet* appears nowhere in the file, or in any

* **Understated:** the guide offered *no* in-Python review path, not an outdated
  one. It presented `suggest_semantics()` → `reviewed.attrs["semantic_suggestions"]`
  → `apply_semantic_suggestions()` and stopped. An outdated path can be followed
  and then corrected; a missing one sends the reader to the reference index to
  assemble the sequence themselves — and `_quarto.yml` had listed all eleven
  calls under *Review and edit (in Python)* since the port landed, so the
  reference already knew what the guide did not say.

metasalmon 0.5.0's NEWS entry leads with reaching
`validate_salmon_datapackage(require_iris = TRUE)` "without opening a single
file in a spreadsheet". That is the claim the guide now has to support, and it
is why the bump could not precede it.
