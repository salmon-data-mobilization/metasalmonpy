  reported as a placeholder still counts as an IRI in the console`. It plants
  `_UNFILLED_IRI_STATES["prose placeholder"]` (`MISSING METADATA:` in
  `tables.csv` `observation_unit_iri`, `REVIEW REQUIRED:` in the
  `spawner_count` row's `unit_iri`) on `filled_package`, a package filled by
  its own printed calls. It makes R's four assertions: the two fields, both

  have them.` It reads the console through `review.render_lines(path_expr="pkg")`,
  as R's reads `.ms_metadata_render_lines(review, path_expr = "pkg")`.
