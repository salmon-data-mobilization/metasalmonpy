## Guards, skips and workarounds added, and what retires each

  upstream spec release; its pin clauses sooner, if the loader stops fetching
  a remote bundle. Written in its docstring.

  its docstring.

  Retires when B-161 is ruled and B-207 emits the ruled pre-1000 spelling. In
  its module docstring.
