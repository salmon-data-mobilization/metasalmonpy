# B-355 — lossless EML instant coverage

## Scope and authority

Port Brett's 2026-09-25 ruling in B-355/B-354: the package's persisted
`YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ` spelling supplies EML's adjacent `calendarDate` and
`time`. The bundled EML 2.2.0 coverage XSD declares `calendarDate` as
`resource:yearDate` (year or date) followed by optional `xs:time`.

`eml._add_coverage()` renders each endpoint once, splits only the complete
ASCII profile instant, and preserves its `Z`. Joining the elements with `T`
recovers the exact CSV cell. Plain year/date values keep one element; mixed
endpoints work independently. Trailing/multiple `T`, fractional/offset time
and a trailing newline keep their whole text in `calendarDate` for validation.
No signature, ontology IRI, frozen row, schema, dependency, version or parity
register changes. R companion: metasalmon PR253 (B-354); neither half is
claimed landed until its merge. This is a port of the ruling, not a new choice.
