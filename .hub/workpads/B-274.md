### The relabel, and the words about the `.cat` accessor

The condition names the defect's mechanism, `rename_categories` on the Series,
and the fix that mechanism suggests. `rename_categories` on the accessor would
run, but it refuses two inputs R's `factor()` accepts. Measured under pandas
3.0.6 and 1.5.3: two codes sharing a label give `ValueError: Categorical
categories must be unique`, where R merges them into one level, and a missing
label gives `ValueError: Categorical categories cannot be null`, where R makes a
missing level. Both would have landed in the `except`, so the column would be
text again with a warning, for a code list R applies. So the relabel does not
call `rename_categories` at all. What the clause is for does hold: the relabel
runs rather than raising, and the one input found that still reaches the
`except` is a repeated column name.
