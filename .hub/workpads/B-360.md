## What changed, and where

- `llm_review.py`, the validator block, from `_VALIDATOR_WEAK_SINGLETONS` to
  `_apply_validators()`. Each function is named after and documented against
  the R function it follows. The block's header comment records the regex
  fact the mirror rests on, measured on 2026-09-25 under R 4.5.2: R's
  `perl = TRUE` patterns run PCRE without Unicode properties, so `\b`, `\s`,
  `\w` and `[[:punct:]]` are ASCII-only, and their mirrors carry `re.ASCII`;
  the four native-type tests R runs in its default engine keep Python's
  Unicode `\b`.
  - `_dimension()`: per-value normalisation (Unicode minus, superscripts,
    middle dot, whitespace); a value that is a whole compound unit decides on
    its own; a time denominator counted more than once or raised to a power
    returns `None`; one denominator adds `rate`; then R's precedence among
    strong physical, rate and weak classes. This package had five classes,
    treated any denominator as rate-or-nothing and matched compound units as
    substrings.
  - `_field_anchors()` and `_chunk_has_anchor()`: the column names are the
    candidates and the labels stand in only without a column name; a candidate
    needs two tokens of three or more characters or six characters that are
    not a weak singleton; a phrase anchor is met only when the chunk starts
    with the phrase after leading ASCII markup and its leading token carries no
    `_` or `-`. R's regexes are copied with their quirks and documented.
  - `_has_method_evidence()`: R's two `by … <observer|technician|…>` and
    `by … <model|algorithm|estimator|…>` alternatives, and `caliper` as R
    spells it (`call?iper` also matched `calliper` here).
  - `_split_role_hints()`: `|` only, trimmed, order and case kept, a
    comma-joined hint one hint; the mismatch message lists hints in that order.
  - `_current_selected_iris()`: the slot IRIs the dictionary already holds (a
    `REVIEW:`-marked value does not count) overridden by the accepted rows, so
    a `CatchAbundance` the dictionary carries pairs with a newly accepted
    `CatchContext`.
  - `_validator_text()`: non-blank values only, untrimmed, one space, lowercase.
  - `_apply_validators()`: findings in R's order (method, constraint, modifier,
    role type, dimension, pair, redundancy); a row whose role has no target is
    skipped rather than raising; the rationale is trimmed as R trims it before
    the notes are appended.
- `tests/test_validator_parity.py` (new, 285 tests) and
  `tests/data/validator_parity/` (`cases.json`, `expected-from-r.R`,
  `expected.json`).
- `.github/workflows/parity.yml`: the parity job runs
  `tests/test_*_parity.py` beside the roundtrip test. B-364 (#55) makes the

- `CHANGELOG.md` under `## Unreleased`, and `AGENTS.md`'s suite counts.

## Found, not absorbed

1. **Candidate (metasalmon): `.ms_semantic_validator_chunk_has_anchor()`'s
   leading-token regex fails on a multi-line chunk.** The pattern
   `^\s*([a-zA-Z0-9][a-zA-Z0-9_-]*).*$` runs under PCRE without `DOTALL`, so on
   a chunk with a newline before its end it does not match, `sub()` returns the
   whole chunk as the leading token, and any `_` or `-` anywhere in the chunk
   defeats the phrase anchor. With 2200-character chunks that is the common
   case; the identifier anchor still works. Copied here deliberately and pinned
   by the `multiline_chunk_phrase_anchor_quirk` case, so a fix in R flips the
   fixture and has to land on both sides in one step.
2. **`_target_key()` uses nine columns where R's group key has eight**, and
   `_dictionary_row()` matches by string equality where
   `.ms_semantic_bundle_dictionary_row()` skips a missing target value. Neither
   changes a verdict on the shared cases; both are pre-existing.
3. **`semantics.py`'s `_split_role_hints()` already splits on `|`**, so
   surface 3 of the role contract needed nothing; the `[|,;]` split lived only
   in the validators.
